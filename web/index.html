Forklift load volume

Estimates the volume of the load on a passing forklift from one camera, using a EUR pallet as the ruler. Follow the four steps.

1The feed

Seven seconds from the source video (2:16 to 2:23). The camera stands still, so it is treated as a camera feed.

The program reads this clip frame by frame. Because the camera does not move, anything that changes is the vehicle. It finds when the truck is in view and which frames are best to measure.

2Mark the six corners

The yellow dots are the corners of the crate stack (pallets excluded), marked by hand on two frames. Drag any dot to correct it. This is the one manual step.

4Where it fails

  • Depth is the weakest number. The side of the load is seen at a steep angle, so a few pixels of error move depth by 10 cm or more. Drag a far corner and refit to see it.
  • The top and back are never seen. A full rectangular stack is assumed.
  • It needs a known-size object. No pallet in view means no scale.
  • Corners are marked by hand. Automatic corner finding is the first thing to add; the backlight in this clip makes it hard.
  • The pass detector assumes a static camera.
  • On a fixed, calibrated camera the focal length, floor and scale are known in advance, so one frame is enough and the depth range shrinks.
